Fantasy Baseball League Analysis

A private, non-commercial MLB fantasy baseball companion dashboard built for personal and single-league analysis.

Overview

Fantasy Baseball League Analysis is a private web dashboard used to analyze teams, matchups, and player performance within our personal Yahoo Fantasy Baseball league during the MLB offseason and regular season.

It pairs our league's scoring settings, standings, and rosters with public MLB statistical splits, park factors, and Statcast metrics so league managers can evaluate roster and matchup decisions in one place.

Yahoo Fantasy Sports API Usage

This application uses Yahoo OAuth 2.0 strictly with Read-Only (fspt-r) permissions:

  • League Discovery & Settings: Reading MLB league configuration, roster positions, and scoring categories (/users;use_login=1/games;game_codes=mlb/leagues, /league/{league_key}/settings).
  • Standings & Matchups: Viewing current league standings and weekly head-to-head matchup scoreboards (/standings, /scoreboard).
  • Rosters & Player Availability: Reading team rosters, injury designations, and league player ownership status (/team/{team_key}/roster, /league/{league_key}/players, /transactions).
  • Low Request Volume & Read-Only: Responses are cached server-side to minimize API requests. The application performs no write operations and no automated transactions.

Intended User Base & Privacy

  • Single-League Personal Scope: Access is strictly limited to personal use and members of our private MLB fantasy league (1–12 users). It is not publicly distributed on app stores or offered as a commercial service.
  • Data Handling & Privacy: OAuth tokens are encrypted/stored privately for authenticated session refresh only. Yahoo Fantasy Sports data is never sold, shared, or redistributed publicly.
